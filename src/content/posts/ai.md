A few other points to touch on:
- There's a lot of discussion about AI's impact on building software...but not as much on maintaining software. What does it mean for human understanding of a codebase if when the first thing goes wrong, we still reach for the LLM to debug? This shakes so many fundamentals about software maintainability and design when you don’t need humans to understand the codebase.
- One big argument around still needing software devs is “but you’ll need them when the thing breaks”. Will they? How important is it to really understand how the thing works anymore? At least from a business standpoint (or what the C-suite thinks is good enough...), not a craft standpoint.
